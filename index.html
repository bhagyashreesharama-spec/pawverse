WHY PAWVERSE EXISTS

A safer world begins with one act of compassion.

Animals experience fear, pain, hunger, loneliness and love just like every living being. Yet countless animals are ignored, injured, abandoned or treated as if their lives matter less.

PAWVERSE is being built as a compassionate collective where people can come together to protect animals and give them safety, care and a chance at a better life.

ABOUT PAWVERSE

We believe compassion should have a place to live.

PAWVERSE is an animal welfare collective created with one simple belief: every life deserves kindness.

The idea comes from seeing animals living on streets, getting injured, being abandoned and sometimes being treated without compassion. Instead of simply looking away, PAWVERSE aims to create a community that notices, responds and cares.

Our work is centered around animals — their safety, welfare, rescue support, awareness, responsible care and helping people become part of the solution.

OUR MOTIVE

Protect. Care. Speak up. Create safer possibilities for animals and encourage people to choose compassion.

THE PERSON BEHIND PAWVERSE

Bhagyashri Sharma

PAWVERSE was born from a desire to create something meaningful for animals — something rooted in compassion rather than indifference.

Seeing animals struggling on streets, being injured or left without care can be difficult to ignore. PAWVERSE is an attempt to turn that feeling into action by bringing together people, skills, awareness and genuine care.

The journey starts small, but the intention is simple: protect, care and speak up.
