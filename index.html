ABOUT PAWVERSE

More Than A Platform. A Community With Purpose.

Pawverse is created with the idea of bringing people and possibilities together through animal-focused initiatives, creative work and meaningful opportunities.

Whether you are a student looking for experience, someone searching for opportunities, a skilled professional, a business or an institution — Pawverse aims to create a space where everyone can contribute.

MEET THE FOUNDER

Bhagyashri Sharma

Founder, Pawverse

Pawverse was born from a simple belief: compassion and opportunity can exist together.

My vision is to create a platform where people from different backgrounds can find ways to learn, contribute and grow — while building a more caring world for animals.

You don't need to know everything before you begin. Sometimes the first step is simply being willing to learn and help.
