THE BEGINNING Care begins with noticing.
WHY I STARTED PAWVERSE

Because caring should have a place.

Pawverse began from a simple thought: there are many people who care about animals, but not everyone knows where they can begin or how they can contribute.

Someone may want to volunteer. Someone may want to help with rescue support. Someone may have skills in design, writing, photography, technology or social media. Someone else may simply want to learn how to care for animals more responsibly.

Pawverse is my attempt to bring those possibilities closer together and create a space where compassion can meet opportunity.

“

You do not always need to do something huge. Sometimes, choosing to care is where everything begins.

MY MESSAGE

I want Pawverse to become a place to begin.

My hope for Pawverse is to create something that feels welcoming rather than intimidating — a place where someone can arrive with an idea, a skill, a question, or simply the desire to help.

It is a growing vision, and its future can be shaped by the people who choose to become part of it.

Bhagyashri Sharma Founder, Pawverse Delhi, India
