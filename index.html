01 / ABOUT

More than a website. A growing idea.

PAWVERSE is being developed as a community-driven animal-welfare initiative from Delhi, India.

The idea is simple: people should have meaningful ways to help animals, regardless of their profession, age or experience.

PAWVERSE aims to bring animal care, awareness, responsible action, community participation and people's skills together.

The journey is still beginning. Future work, stories and impact will be documented honestly as they happen.

06 / FOUNDER

Bhagyashri Sharma

Founder, PAWVERSE

PAWVERSE started from a simple thought: caring about animals should not stop at feeling sorry for them. People need meaningful ways to understand, participate and act.

The vision is to bring animal welfare, community participation, awareness and people's skills together in one growing initiative.

This journey is still at its beginning. PAWVERSE will grow through learning, real participation and genuine work — not through made-up numbers or stories.
