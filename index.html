ABOUT PAWVERSE

One community.
Many ways to help.

PAWVERSE is being built as a community where people can come together to create meaningful change in the lives of animals.

From rescuing a street animal to helping with technology, design, medical care, transportation, adoption or content — every contribution can matter.

WHAT WE DO

From the street
to a second chance.

Our future work will focus on practical help, awareness and creating better lives for animals.

Rescue

Helping animals that are injured, abandoned, trapped or in need of immediate support.

Food & Care

Supporting street animals with food, clean water and basic care.

Medical Help

Connecting animals in need with appropriate medical assistance.

Shelter

Working towards safe spaces where animals can recover and feel protected.

Adoption

Helping animals find responsible and loving forever homes.

Awareness

Creating content that encourages people to understand and care for animals.

MEET THE FOUNDER

Bhagyashri Sharma

Founder, PAWVERSE

I believe that helping animals shouldn't be limited to people with money, professional skills or a particular background.

Everyone has something they can contribute.

My motive behind PAWVERSE is to create a community where people can use their skills, time and willingness to learn to make a meaningful difference in the lives of animals.

PAWVERSE aims to bring together people from different backgrounds — developers, designers, creators, medical professionals, drivers, volunteers, students and anyone who simply wants to help.

“One person. One skill. One act of kindness. One life changed.”
