Our Mission

Small acts of kindness can create a safer world for every living being.

PAWVERSE aims to create awareness, encourage responsible animal care and build a community where street animals are seen, heard and cared for.

RESCUE Help animals in difficult situations and connect them with people who can help.
CARE Promote food, shelter, medical care and compassion.
AWARENESS Encourage people to understand and respect animals.
