01 / ABOUT PAWVERSE

WHAT IS PAWVERSE?

One vision.
Every species.

PAWVERSE is an emerging animal-welfare initiative being built from Delhi, India.

Our purpose is to gradually create a community and practical network around animals who are vulnerable, injured, abandoned or in need of care.

Our vision includes dogs, cats, cows, birds, monkeys and other animals — because compassion should not be limited by species.

06 / THE PERSON BEHIND PAWVERSE
FOUNDER Bhagyashree Sharma

FOUNDER, PAWVERSE

I don't believe
compassion should
have a species limit.

I started PAWVERSE with a simple belief: every life deserves compassion.

Animals cannot always ask for help when they are hungry, injured, abandoned or vulnerable. I want to build something that encourages people to look beyond simply feeling sorry for them and instead find responsible ways to help.

At the same time, I believe there are people who want to work and learn but may not have formal education, professional skills, experience or the money to access expensive training.

I want PAWVERSE to eventually create meaningful pathways where people can learn, contribute, gain practical exposure and discover what they are capable of.

I don't want to build PAWVERSE by making promises that cannot be kept. I want to build it gradually, honestly and responsibly — starting with a clear purpose and growing through real people, real work and real experiences.

“Start small. Build honestly. Let real work create the story.”
