ABOUT PAWVERSE

A small beginning
with a big heart.

PAWVERSE is an animal welfare initiative built around compassion, awareness, responsible action and community.

OUR PURPOSE

Giving animals a voice through compassion and action.

PAWVERSE was created from a simple thought: animals deserve kindness, safety and respect, regardless of where they come from or what they look like.

The vision is to build a community where people can come together to support animals in meaningful ways — through rescue support, awareness, creativity, volunteering, responsible care and everyday acts of kindness.

PAWVERSE is intended to grow step by step, with people who genuinely want to contribute and learn along the way.

MEET THE FOUNDER

Bhagyashri Sharma

Founder & Director

Delhi, India

PAWVERSE is my attempt to create a space where compassion becomes action.

I believe helping animals does not always require a special title, a professional degree or a large organisation. Sometimes it begins with simply noticing an animal in need and choosing not to look away.

My vision for PAWVERSE is to bring together people with different abilities, backgrounds and skills who share one thing — the willingness to help.

With compassion,
Bhagyashri Sharma

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Values before everything else.

Our foundation is built around a few simple principles.

01

Kindness

Every animal deserves to be treated with patience, dignity and care.

02

Responsibility

Helping animals also means learning to act responsibly and thoughtfully.

03

Community

Different people can contribute in different ways, and every genuine contribution matters.

04

Respect

We believe animals should be respected as living beings, not treated as disposable.
